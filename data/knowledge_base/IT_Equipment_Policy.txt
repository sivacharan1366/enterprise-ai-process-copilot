IT EQUIPMENT POLICY

Document ID: IT-POL-001
Version: 1.0

1. Laptop Requests

Employees may request a company laptop when their current equipment is unavailable, damaged, outdated, or insufficient for their assigned responsibilities.

2. Request Process

A laptop request must include:
- Employee name
- Department
- Reason for the request
- Required specifications

3. Approval Process

Laptop requests require approval from the employee's reporting manager.

Requests for high-performance workstations may require additional approval from the IT department.

4. Processing

After approval, the IT department reviews the request and determines the appropriate equipment based on the employee's role and technical requirements.

5. Replacement

Employees may request replacement equipment when the existing device is damaged or no longer meets the requirements of their role.

6. Security

Company equipment must be used according to the organization's security policies. Employees must not install unauthorized software or disable security controls.

7. Support

Employees experiencing hardware or software problems should contact the IT support team.
